# Example Plumbing — First 30 Days

*Draft for internal review. Prepared Oct 12, 2026.*

## Where you are

You have the best rating in the [City] plumber results — 5.0 across 82 reviews — and the fewest reviews of any established competitor. Competitor A, Competitor B and Competitor C each have over 1,000. Your site currently ranks #3 for "plumber [City] [State]," which is strong for the review count, but the gap in volume will widen without a process.

Your site already does the important things: real phone number, real address, real reviews, real project photos. The copy is honest and the structured data is mostly right.

## What we'd do in the first 30 days

**1. Review velocity.** A simple ask at job completion — text link, one tap. Target: 10 new reviews in 30 days. Doesn't touch the rating; just the count.

**2. Fix the structured data.** Remove the Google reviews from your homepage markup (Google treats reviews you host about yourself as self-serving and won't show stars for them). Add a founding date, a logo, and links from each service in the markup to its page. One afternoon of dev work.

**3. Fix the service area copy.** Your homepage says "serving every corner of [Town 6]," which is a template error. Your about page lists ten communities including [Town 9] and [Town 10]; your homepage lists eight. Pick one list and use it everywhere.

**4. Expand availability messaging.** Every competitor in the results shows 24-hour availability. Add "available 24/7 for emergencies" to your Google profile and homepage hero so you're not the only one who looks closed at 6pm.

**5. Start a homeowner how-to series.** Short DIY guides — "how to shut off your water main," "how to reset a water heater" — build trust and rank for high-volume queries. Three per month.

**6. Get into the septic results.** You offer septic install, repair, pumping and inspection; the septic results are a different, thinner set of businesses, and you're not in them yet. Add the septic category to your Google profile and strengthen the septic pages. This is the cheapest new visibility available to you.

## What we'd measure

Calls. Not rankings. You're built for calls — every button on your site is a phone number or a free-estimate form — so that's the number.
