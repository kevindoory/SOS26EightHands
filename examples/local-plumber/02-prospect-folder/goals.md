# Goals — Example Plumbing INC

> **This file is a proposal.** Prospect file: these are goals we'd *propose* based on what's observable, not goals Example Plumbing has stated. Each one separates what we observed from what we'd propose. After the first call, their words replace this.

## What the site is built to do
Every call to action is "get a free estimate," "schedule a free inspection," or the phone number. `[site:home]` Two conversion paths: phone, and the estimate form. No booking tool, no pricing, no chat seen on the captured pages. `[site:home]`

## What we'd propose

### 1. Review volume
**Observed:** Example Plumbing is tied for the highest displayed rating in the captured plumber set (5.0, with Competitor I) and has 83 reviews. Eight of the ten other businesses have more; the three largest have over 1,000. `[local-results]` `[gbp]` The five most recent profile reviews describe what their copy claims: on time, explains the work, handles permits. `[gbp]`
**Proposed:** a review-ask step at job completion. Measured as review count over time.
**Not claimed:** that rating and count measure service quality, or that the rating will hold as the count grows.

### 2. The hours story
**Observed:** Nine of the ten other plumbers display "Open 24 hours"; Competitor J displays 24-hour weekday hours and limited Saturday hours. Example Plumbing displays Mon–Fri 8–5 and is the only listing explicitly showing both Saturday and Sunday closed. `[local-results]` `[gbp]` Example Plumbing's FAQ: emergency calls are handled "during business hours Monday through Friday, 8am to 5pm." `[site:home]` Two blog posts on the index are titled "24/7 Plumbing in [Town 8], [State]" (dated October 2, 2026) and "24 Hour Emergency Plumbing Service in [Town 3], [State]" (dated October 4, 2026). `[site:blog]`
**Proposed:** first conversation. Either they offer after-hours and should say so everywhere, or they don't and the blog shouldn't promise it. We don't know which; we know the site currently says both.

### 3. The differentiators
**Observed:** Example Plumbing offers septic install/repair/pumping/inspection, excavation, hydro excavation, live water tapping, water treatment. `[site:home]` The septic results are a different set of businesses with lower review counts (12 to 89), and Example Plumbing did not appear among them in this capture. `[local-results]` GBP categories returned were plumber and general contractor — no septic category. `[gbp]`
**Proposed:** evaluate whether GBP category and septic service-page improvements could improve visibility for septic searches, where they don't currently appear.
**Not claimed:** that this set is easier to rank in. Fewer businesses with fewer reviews is what we saw; demand and difficulty weren't measured.

### 4. The blog
**Observed:** Ten posts dated Oct 2–11, 2026, every title "[topic] in [city], [State]," every one tagged Buyer's Guide. `[site:blog]` Two promise 24-hour service (see 2). One is a DIY how-to: "How to Flush a Toilet With No Water." `[site:blog]` The blog index intro ends with a stray "[Town 6]." `[site:blog]`
**Proposed:** ask who produces the posts and whether they're reviewed before publishing; fix the two that contradict the hours; decide on purpose whether DIY content fits a call-driven business.
**Not claimed:** how the posts are produced. "Highly templated" is observable; "programmatic" or "AI-written" is a guess.

## What a win would look like (proposed)
More calls. The site is built for calls and a form, so that's the measure. `[site:home]` Rankings would be a secondary check.

## What they measure
Unknown. Ask on the first call whether they track calls by source.
