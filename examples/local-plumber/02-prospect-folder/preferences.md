## Choices we can observe

- **No pricing on the captured pages.** Every CTA is "free estimate" or "free inspection." `[site:home]` Schema says `priceRange: "$$"`. `[site:schema]`
- **No online booking on the captured pages.** Phone and email are the contact methods shown. `[site:home]` `[site:about]` Contact page not captured.
- **Published hours are Mon–Fri 8–5.** Site and GBP agree. `[site:home]` `[gbp]` The FAQ says emergency calls are handled "during business hours Monday through Friday, 8am to 5pm." `[site:home]` Whether anyone answers after 5 is not something a page can tell us.
- **Trust language, not price language.** "Honest" and "straightforward" appear on both captured pages; "no pressure" on the homepage. `[site:home]` `[site:about]` "Affordable" and "cheap" don't appear in the site copy on those two pages; "affordable" appears in one customer review. `[site:home]` `[gbp]`
- **Project photos in the portfolio.** Four named projects with images. `[site:home]` Whether the images are of those jobs wasn't verified.
- **Reviews front and center.** Both captured pages carry a five-review block. `[site:home]` `[site:about]`
- **Family and local as the identity.** "Family-run," "[City] native," "hometown." `[site:home]` `[site:about]`
