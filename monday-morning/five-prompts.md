# Monday morning — five prompts

Paste these, in order, into whatever AI you use. By the end you'll have mapped one of your deliverables and built a rung 1 tool for the worst part of it. Takes about twenty minutes, plus a test.

They work as-is in Claude, ChatGPT, or anything else. One conversation, five turns.

---

## 1 — Name it

> Here's a deliverable I produce regularly for clients: **[describe it — what it is, who it's for, how often, roughly how long it takes you]**. Before you do anything else, ask me questions until you understand it end to end — the inputs, the steps, what "done" looks like, and where it usually goes wrong. Ask them one at a time.

Answer the questions. Be honest about the parts you skip.

## 2 — Map the hands

> Now list every step from start to send, as a numbered list. Include the steps I probably skip when I'm busy — research before, QA after, remembering what this client said last time. One line each. Don't group them.

Read the list. Add anything it missed.

## 3 — Sort them

> For each step, answer two things: (a) does it need my judgment or my relationship with the client — yes or no, with one reason; (b) if no, what shape of AI hand fits: a **prompt** (one job, same way every time), a **workflow with memory** (needs to know the client), or an **agent** (runs on its own on a trigger). Put it in a table.

Push back where it's wrong. It'll mark too many steps as needing you.

## 4 — Pick one

> Of the steps that don't need me, which single one would save the most time or gets skipped most often? Pick one and tell me why. Then write me the prompt for it: a fixed input, a fixed output format I can skim, numbered instructions, and a line telling it not to explain or pad.

Save the prompt somewhere.

## 5 — Test it

> Here are two of these I did by hand. For each one I'm pasting the original inputs and what I delivered: **[set 1: the inputs + my finished version] [set 2: the inputs + my finished version]**. Run your prompt on each set of inputs. For each, show me side by side every place your output disagrees with mine. Don't tell me which is better — just list the differences.

Decide who's right — check each difference against the source, not against which version sounds better. Fix the prompt where it's the prompt. Now you have a rung 1 tool.

---

## Then

Do prompt 2's list again next month for a different deliverable. The hands that show up on every list are the ones worth a rung 2 folder (`02-memory/`). The QA hand that never gets done is rung 3 (`03-agent/`).

*v2 — Oct 12, 2026. Prompt 5 now asks for the original inputs, not just your finished work — the AI can't redo a task from the answers alone.*
