# Goals — [client name]

## What they're paying for, in their words
> The thing they said on the sales call. Not "increase organic traffic." "More calls from people who aren't price-shopping."

## This quarter
> One to three measurable things. Date them.

## This year
> Where the business is trying to go. Expanding service area? New line of work? Selling in three years?

## What a win looks like to them
> What they'd mention to a friend if it happened.

## What they measure (and what they don't)
> If they only look at phone calls, rankings reports are noise. Write it down.
