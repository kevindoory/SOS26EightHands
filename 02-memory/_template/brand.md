# Brand — [client name]

## Who they are, in one paragraph
> How they'd describe themselves to a neighbor. Not the mission statement.

## Voice
> Three or four adjectives, then one sentence of what that sounds like. "Plain, confident, a little dry. Never exclamation points."

## Say this
> Phrases, product names, and positioning they use and want used.

## Never say this
> Words and claims that are off limits — legal, competitive, or just the owner hates them.

## Proof points
> Years in business, licenses, awards, numbers they're proud of and can back up.
